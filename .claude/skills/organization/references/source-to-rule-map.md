# Source-To-Rule Map

| Rule ID | Production rule | Basis | Derivation |
|---|---|---|---|
| OR-01 | Keep the parent at selection, ordering, contracts, gap reporting, and recording recipient-declared completion. | local architecture; O1; C1 | Progressive disclosure prevents a hidden workshop, rollout, or child-artifact review method. |

## Audit Notes

- Source presence does not prove derivation; read the stated limits.
- No source here supports universal reviewer identities, gate records, pilot status, rollback fields, benchmark suites, or iteration counters for every organization route.
- Examples such as workshops, MCP, CI, communities of practice, or Skills are not mandatory channels unless the request supplies that constraint.
