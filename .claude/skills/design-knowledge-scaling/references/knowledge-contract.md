# Design Knowledge Scaling Contract

Apply this contract to one claimed design practice or slogan and one reuse decision. Qualification determines whether it is source-backed; empty fields do not establish applicability, authority, or adoption.

## Qualification

Distinguish:

- `source-claim`: stated by an identified practice source;
- `observed-use`: directly evidenced use, failure, or misunderstanding in a named context;
- `inferred-applicability`: reasoned extension beyond observed contexts;
- `local-proposal`: a proposed packaging or channel decision;
- `contradicted`: incompatible current evidence;
- `unknown`: not established.

Do not scale an unsourced slogan or one person's preference as a practice. A not-ready decision should preserve the evidence needed to reconsider it.

## Channel-Neutral Knowledge Contract

```text
Intended audience and recurring problem:
Source and target contexts:
Constraints:
Practice and rationale:
Inputs and preconditions:
Expected output:
Completion condition:
Applicability and exclusions:
Failure or misuse modes:
Stable meaning:
Permitted contextual variation:
Provenance and authority:
Consequential unknowns:
```

Use only material fields. A reusable unit may stand alone or relate to other units, but do not split or combine it merely to fit a packaging taxonomy.

## Channel Fit

- `reference-or-template`: meaning is sufficiently codifiable and retrieval or adaptation is the primary need;
- `skill-candidate`: a repeatable, directly invocable task has a clear trigger, inputs, workflow boundary, output, and completion;
- `human-exchange`: causal ambiguity, tacit judgment, sensitivity, or contextual variation makes dialogue central;
- `hybrid`: a stable codified contract and bounded human interpretation are both material;
- `no-dissemination-yet`: evidence, authority, applicability, or source-of-truth is not ready.

Compare only factors material to the decision: recurrence, codifiability, ambiguity, contextual variation, recipient capability, reach, tool coupling, update cost, authority, and sensitivity. Do not choose a channel because it is fashionable or already installed.

## Maintenance And Feedback

Record the current practice source, source-of-truth, or unresolved maintenance responsibility. Identify actual-use evidence that could reveal misunderstanding, contradiction, source change, or changed applicability. Use evidenced events, not a mandatory cadence or KPI program, to reopen the decision.

Packaging is not adoption. Publication, invocation, or attendance does not prove correct use, transfer, maintenance, or outcome.

## Status Test

Use `scaling-decision-supported` for an evidence-linked channel choice or `no-dissemination-yet` result whose limits are usable for the named decision. Missing support that justifies this usable not-ready fit is not itself incomplete. Use `scaling-decision-incomplete` only when a material practice, context, authority, or fit distinction cannot be bounded.
