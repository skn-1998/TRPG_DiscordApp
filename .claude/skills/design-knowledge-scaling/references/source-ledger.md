# Source Ledger

No Qiita, Zenn, or quotation inherited from either is used. MinoDriven supplies the primary modular scaling intent; original research and official guidance constrain experience packaging, channel fit, transfer, maintenance, and Skill candidacy.

## MinoDriven Primary Sources

### M1. Effective Learning of Good Code

- URL: https://speakerdeck.com/minodriven/effective-learning-of-good-code
- Verified: 2026-07-16; relevant slides 18, 20, 26, 33, 36-42, and 45-50.
- Adopted: practical design learning includes recognizing a problem, improving an artifact, satisfying structural needs, and explaining why the decision addresses the problem.
- Limit: one DMM learning format with self-reported outcomes. It does not define cross-team packaging, governance, or adoption.

### M2. Modifius

- URL: https://speakerdeck.com/minodriven/modifius
- Verified: 2026-07-16; relevant slides 16, 20-26, 43-50, and 52-55.
- Adopted: one expert could not directly support every team; the reported implementation separates a stable changeability basis from task functions, architecture or framework viewpoints, and language support, selecting prompts by situation.
- Limit: proprietary prompts and one organization case. MCP, the module taxonomy, speed claims, testimonials, and slide 44's channel choice are contextual, not universal prescriptions. The linked Qiita event source is excluded.

## Supporting Sources

### S1. Basili, Caldiera, and Rombach, The Experience Factory (Encyclopedia of Software Engineering, 1994)

- URL: https://www.cs.umd.edu/~basili/publications/technical/T88.pdf
- Relevant printed pages: 469-474 (PDF pages 1-6).
- Adopted: experience must be characterized, analyzed, packaged, contextualized, and tailored before reuse; feedback can update packages.
- Limit: the full model's separate organization, measurement program, repository, funding, and education structure is outside this Skill.

### S2. Meszaros and Doble, Pattern Writing

- URL: https://europlop.net/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/pattern-writing-patterns.pdf
- Relevant PDF pages: 3-8, 23-24, and 33.
- Adopted: a reusable unit can preserve problem, context, forces, solution, rationale, and audience; complex methods can be factored into related bounded units.
- Limit: pattern form is one packaging grammar, not a requirement that every learning become a pattern language.

### S3. Hansen, Nohria, and Tierney, Knowledge Strategy

- URL: https://www.library.hbs.edu/working-knowledge/whats-your-strategy-for-managing-knowledge
- Adopted: codification supports creator-independent retrieval and reuse, while personalization supports knowledge requiring dialogue and contextual judgment.
- Limit: consulting-firm comparison, not a universal ratio, staffing model, incentive, or investment prescription.

### S4. Wenger-Trayner, Communities of Practice

- URL: https://www.wenger-trayner.com/introduction-to-communities-of-practice/
- Adopted: shared practice can carry experiences, stories, tools, and recurring-problem knowledge, including tacit or changing knowledge; there is no single recipe or best technology.
- Limit: does not require a community, leader, facilitator, workshop, cadence, or platform.

### S5. Szulanski, Internal Stickiness

- URL: https://sms.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/smj.4250171105
- Relevant pages: 27-43.
- Adopted: recipient absorptive capacity, causal ambiguity, and difficult source-recipient relationships can impede transfer; publication alone does not establish successful adoption.
- Limit: eight-company 1990s study; it does not prescribe a dissemination channel or modern tool architecture.

### S6. KCS v6 Practices Guide

- URLs: https://library.serviceinnovation.org/KCS/KCS_v6/KCS_v6_Practices_Guide/030 and https://library.serviceinnovation.org/KCS/KCS_v6/KCS_v6_Practices_Guide/095
- Adopted: knowledge should be findable and usable by its audience; context is best captured near use; reuse can expose maintenance needs.
- Limit: KCS roles, states, adoption phases, measures, and customer-support program are not adopted. Only use-triggered maintenance evidence is relevant.

### S7. OpenAI Codex Manual, Skills

- URL: https://developers.openai.com/codex/codex-manual.md
- Adopted: Skills are reusable workflows with clear metadata scope, explicit invocation, and progressive loading of references or scripts.
- Limit: packaging and runtime guidance only; it does not validate design knowledge or determine organizational fit.

- Qualify evidence before packaging and permit a complete no-dissemination-yet result.
- Use a channel-neutral contract rather than a fixed module taxonomy.
- Compare codified, interpersonal, hybrid, Skill-candidate, and not-ready fits.
- Keep maintenance responsibility unresolved when no source or authority is supplied.
