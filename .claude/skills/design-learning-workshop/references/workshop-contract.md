# Design Learning Workshop Contract

Apply this contract to one named design capability. Empty fields do not create learning needs, authority, or evidence.

## Capability Frame

```text
Capability and source:
Problem the capability addresses:
Practitioners and current practice:
Observable capability-relevant action:
Artifact options and authenticity limits:
Constraints and unknowns:
```

Do not build a workshop from an unsourced design slogan. A capability may be provisional only when its source and unresolved authority remain explicit.

## Artifact Decision

Prefer an artifact close enough to the participants' work to expose the named problem. Use anonymized, synthetic, or safely representative material when production artifacts are unavailable, confidential, unsafe, or inaccessible. Record what the substitute cannot establish about workplace transfer.

## Practice Loop

Use only elements needed for the capability and decision:

```text
Attempt: capability-relevant work each intended learner can perform
Explanation: problem, purpose, decision, alternatives, and expected effect
Feedback: evidence about the task, reasoning, or process
Revision: response to material feedback, when useful
Session observation: what can be seen or heard during the workshop
Transfer unknown: what requires later workplace evidence
```

This is a semantic loop, not a fixed schedule. It may use code, diagrams, ADRs, scenarios, or another justified design artifact. Group participation alone is not evidence that each learner performed the work.

## Evidence Discipline

- `supplied`: stated by the request or an identified source;
- `observed`: directly shown in current practice or a completed session artifact;
- `inferred`: a bounded explanation derived from evidence;
- `unknown`: not established.

Session evidence may show an attempt, explanation, or revision. It does not by itself show generalization to work or maintenance over time. Do not use learning-pyramid retention percentages or a universal claim that lecture and reading are ineffective.

## Feedback Boundary

Tie feedback to the capability goal, task result, reasoning, or working process. Do not require praise, person-level judgment, ranking, certification, performance review, or a named facilitator. Preserve accessibility and participation constraints when choosing the feedback form.

## Status Test

Use `workshop-design-supported` when another person can run the bounded design without inventing the capability, learning problem, safe artifact, or evidence meaning. Use `workshop-design-incomplete` when a material source, context, safety, or accessibility gap prevents that use.
